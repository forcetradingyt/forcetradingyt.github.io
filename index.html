FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Force Trading YT?
Force Trading YT is a trading education brand that teaches Indian stock market traders practical, rule-based strategies — price action, swing trading, breakouts, and risk management.
What does Premium Membership include?
Premium includes a private community, daily market analysis, educational content, and priority support.
How much does Premium Membership cost?
Premium plans start at ₹99/month, with discounted 3-month, 6-month, and yearly options available on the pricing page.
What is the Trading Scanner?
The Trading Scanner filters thousands of NSE stocks daily and highlights setups that match proven price-action criteria, saving you research time.
Do I need trading experience to use the Scanner?
No. The Scanner is designed to be beginner-friendly, and we also provide guidance on how to read and act on the results.
What topics do the eBooks cover?
Our eBooks cover EMA-based strategies, breakout trading, price action, and risk management, structured step-by-step for practical learning.
Is there a refund policy?
Digital products are generally non-refundable once accessed, since content is delivered instantly. Reach out to our support if you have a specific concern.
What payment methods are accepted?
Payments are processed securely through Superprofile, supporting UPI, cards, and net banking.
How do I join the WhatsApp community?
You can join directly from the floating WhatsApp button on this site or through the Contact section.
How long does it take to learn trading?
It varies by individual, but most traders need 3–6 months of consistent, disciplined practice to build real competence.
Is this suitable for complete beginners?
Yes. Our learning roadmap is designed to take you from the basics of chart reading to consistent, disciplined trading.
Do you guarantee profits?
No. We never guarantee returns. Trading involves risk, and our focus is on teaching a disciplined, educational process — not promising outcomes.
What markets do you focus on?
Our primary focus is the Indian stock market (NSE), covering swing trading, intraday, and positional strategies.
How is risk management taught?
We teach position sizing, stop-loss placement, and capital allocation as core parts of every strategy — not as an afterthought.
How often is content updated?
We publish regular market updates and analysis, and continuously refine our educational material based on current market conditions.
Can I get support if I have questions?
Yes, Premium members get priority support, and general questions can be asked in the WhatsApp community.
Do you use unnecessary indicators?
No. We deliberately avoid indicator clutter and focus on price action, structure, and clean chart reading.
Is the Scanner a one-time purchase or subscription?
Pricing details for the Scanner are available on our pricing page, including one-time and bundle options.
What makes Force Trading YT different?
We focus on honest, practical education with real market examples — no fake screenshots, no unrealistic promises, just disciplined learning.
