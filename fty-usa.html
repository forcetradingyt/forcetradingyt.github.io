How to use FTY Scanner

This is the actual workflow, not theory. Read the first section and you can already run a scan; the rest explains what the numbers mean so you stop guessing.

The 60-second version

start here
  1. Pick your market in the left rail — 🇮🇳 India, 🇺🇸 US or 🔬 Crypto. Each one is a full scanner with its own universe.

03

Reading the table

the numbers

The engine asks one question: has this price been respecting its EMA? Every column is an answer to that, from a different angle.

SCORE 0–100
Eight components added up: respect, interactions, rejections, proximity, EMA slope, structure, continuation, failure control. 70+ is a clean follower, 90+ is near-perfect.
NEVER BROKE
The price never closed on the wrong side of the EMA in the whole respect window. YES is the strongest single flag on the board.
DISTANCE
How far price sits from the EMA, in %. Small = entry is still close. Under 2% is the sweet spot; over 10% means you are chasing.
EMA
Which period the coin is riding. Auto searches every period from 15 to 250 and keeps the one the price actually respected — so you see 47 or 118, not just round numbers. Or fix it yourself (11 / 21 / 51 / 101 / 201, or any period you type).
SETUP
Candle behaviour right at the EMA — Hammer, Pin Bar, Bullish candle, Retest, Rejection. Fresh means it happened on the latest candles.
SIGNAL
STRONG BUY = never broke + score 90+ + fresh + rising EMA. BUY = score 80+ and fresh. WATCH = score 70+. On the SELL side the same ladder reads STRONG SELL / SELL.
24h %
Crypto only — the last 24 hours. On stocks the daily move is already inside the distance and the score.
SECTOR
Which bucket the symbol belongs to, so you can see when five rows are really one trade.
A score is a description, not a promise. It says "this price respected this EMA" — it does not say the trend continues tomorrow. Size positions as if every scan can be wrong.
04

Scan setup

tap BUY · 1H · Auto

These three pills open the setup drawer. It controls what the next scan does — nothing changes until you press Scan again.

SCAN
All 11 TFs fills every timeframe board in one pass (slowest, most complete). Swing · D/W/M is daily/weekly/monthly only. This TF · fast scans just the timeframe you are looking at — use this when you want a quick read.
SIDE
▲ BUY finds price riding above the EMA (support). ▼ SELL finds price riding below it (resistance). One scan fills both sides, so switching is instant.
TIMEFRAME
5m → 1M. Lower = faster decisions, more noise. Higher = slower, more reliable. Most people should live on 1H and 1D.
EMA
Auto tests 16 periods per coin and keeps the cleanest. Or fix one (10/20/50/100/200, or type any period) for a faster, narrower pass.
Fastest useful scan: This TF + Auto. It answers "what is clean on my timeframe right now" in about half a minute.
05

Filters

the Filters pill

Filters never change the scan — they only trim what you are looking at. The scan counts stay honest, the list gets shorter.

MARKET
All / a single exchange (NSE, BSE, NASDAQ, NYSE, AMEX, Binance).
MIN PRICE
Hide everything below a price — useful to drop sub-paisa coins and penny stocks.
STRATEGY
EMA Followers (score 70+), Fresh Setup, Strong Buy, Setup (a candle pattern is present).
SECTOR ROTATION
Sectors ranked by their average 24h move, with how many scanned coins sit in each. Tap one to filter.
SECTORS · CRYPTO
The full sector list (Layer 1, Layer 2, DeFi, Meme, AI, Gaming, RWA…). Same filter, alphabetical.
06

Sector rotation

the heatmap

One row per sector, one column per timeframe, and the cell is that sector's average move on that timeframe. Colour scale is per column, because +0.1% is a big move on 5m and noise on 1M.

ALIGN
The number that matters: how many timeframes are green. 5/7 or better means the sector is trending everywhere — that is rotation. 1/7 means one timeframe had a good hour.
SORT
Click any timeframe chip (or the column header) to re-rank the sectors by that timeframe. Instant — the data is already loaded.
CONSTITUENTS
Click a sector to see every stock inside it, sorted by the selected timeframe.
COPY 30
Copies the next 30 symbols to your clipboard, one per line, ready for your broker or chart. Click again for 31–60, and so on.
How to actually use it: find a sector with high ALIGN, open its constituents, and pick the names that also score well in the scanner. Sector tailwind + clean EMA = the setup worth taking.
07

Order flow

where the money is going

Inflow and outflow, ranked, plus a by-sector breakdown. Two different measurements depending on the market:

CRYPTO
Real order flow. Binance publishes taker buy volume separately, so this is aggressive buying minus aggressive selling, in dollars, over the last 24 hourly candles.
INDIA / US
Proxy. No free tick-level feed for cash equities, so it is turnover × move: a stock up 3% on ₹500 Cr turnover reads +₹15 Cr. Direction is reliable, the absolute number is an estimate.
Read it as a filter, not a signal. Money leaving a sector you were about to buy is a reason to wait. Money piling into a sector with high ALIGN is confirmation, not a trigger.

Three habits

before you risk money
  1. Check the higher timeframe first. A 5m STRONG BUY inside a daily downtrend is a scalp, not a position. Look at 1D before you act on 15m.
  2. Never chase distance. If price is already 12–15% above its EMA, the entry has been and gone. The best rows on this board are the ones at 1–3%.
  3. One scan is one opinion. Run it again after a few hours. If the same names keep showing up, that is a trend. If the list churns completely, the market is chopping and cash is a position.
This tool scans, it does not advise. No score, signal or flow number here is a recommendation to buy or sell. Verify on the chart, size the position so a wrong call is survivable, and use the paper terminal until the workflow is muscle memory.
