📖 How to use with Chartink, TradingView Screener & other scanners

Show / Hide

Follow these steps to copy symbols from any online stock scanner and import them into TradingView watchlists:

  1. Open your scanner – e.g., Chartink, TradingView Screener, Screener.in, or any other.
  2. Select the symbols you want (you can filter or sort as needed).
  3. Copy the symbol column – most scanners let you select and copy the list of symbols (e.g., “RELIANCE”, “TCS”, etc.).
  4. Paste them into the text area below (comma, space, or newline separated).
  5. Click “Split into Batches” – the tool will automatically divide them into groups of 30 (TradingView free limit).
  6. For each batch, click “Copy Batch” and paste it directly into a new TradingView watchlist.
